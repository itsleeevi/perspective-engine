# How Tesla Took Over the Electric Car Industry

Second How They Took Over cut. Do not clone this rise onto another company.

## Spine

Transformation, not a car biography. `the_thought`: **They sold a sports car so they could build the charging road.** Cold open: one white Supercharger stall on a 2012 rest stop, then the industry parks on that road. Silent cards: **Sports Car First** → **Dead Range** → **Six Stalls** → **Factory Tent** → **Own Store** → **Other Plugs** → **Best Seller** → **Open Road**.

The rise is the 2006 Master Plan ladder (sports car pays for a cheaper car), six California Supercharger stalls on September 24, 2012 that would not even charge Tesla's own Roadster, the 2018 factory tent that pushed Model 3 through 5,031 cars in one week, then Ford's May 25, 2023 NACS decision to use 12,000 Superchargers. FY2025 filings: 1,636,129 deliveries; Model Y the best-selling vehicle of any kind globally; automotive revenue down year over year. Signature prop: one white charging stall. Related tease: the cell plants that pour the powder.
