# How Apple Won the Smartphone War

How They Took Over cut. Do not clone this rise onto another company.

## Spine

Transformation, not a handset biography. `the_thought`: **They took the keys off the phone, then rented the empty glass to other people's software.** Cold open: a slab of empty glass where a keypad used to live. Silent cards: **No Keypad** → **Web Cage** → **Native Dock** → **Cheap Door** → **Glass Rent** → **Court Trim** → **Services Pile** → **Open Glass**.

The rise is January 9, 2007 iPhone (one mechanical button; fingers as the pointing device), a first year of web apps, then March 6, 2008 SDK and 70/30 App Store, July 10–11 2008 500+ native apps and the $199 3G door. FY2025 10-K (year ended September 27, 2025): total $416,161 million; iPhone $209,586 million; Services $109,158 million at 75.4% gross margin versus Products 36.8%. Signature prop: one empty-glass phone with one home button and no keypad. Related tease: the feed that fills a face without asking for friends first.
