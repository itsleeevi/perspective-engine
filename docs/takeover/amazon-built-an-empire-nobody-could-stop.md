## Spine

Transformation, not a retail biography. `the_thought`: **They let other stores move into their warehouse, then the warehouse became the empire.** Cold open: a plain brown carton in a yellow tote that is someone else's store. Silent cards: **Day One Books** → **Two Misses** → **Other Shelves** → **Pack Their Boxes** → **Membership Lock** → **Extra Computer** → **Ads On Shelf** → **Hard To Leave**.

The rise is the 1997 Day 1 book catalog, Auctions and zShops as misses, Marketplace on the same page, Fulfillment by Amazon on September 19, 2006, then Prime, AWS, and ads as stacked rent. FY2025 10-K: $716.924 billion net sales; $172.162 billion third-party seller services; $68.635 billion ads; $128.725 billion AWS. Signature prop: one brown carton in a yellow tote. Related tease: warehouse robotics.
